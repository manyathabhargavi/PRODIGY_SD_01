Conversion formulas

The exact formulas Tempora applies for every conversion.

Celsius → Fahrenheit

°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32.

Celsius → Kelvin

K = °C + 273.15

Add 273.15 to the Celsius value.

Fahrenheit → Celsius

°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Subtract 32, then multiply the result by 5/9.

Fahrenheit → Kelvin

K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Convert to Celsius first, then add 273.15.

Kelvin → Celsius

°C = K − 273.15

Subtract 273.15 from the Kelvin value.

Kelvin → Fahrenheit

°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Convert to Celsius first, then to Fahrenheit.

Units

About the three units

Celsius (°C)

Based on water's freezing point at 0° and boiling point at 100° under standard atmospheric pressure. The everyday scale in most of the world.

Fahrenheit (°F)

Water freezes at 32° and boils at 212°. Commonly used for weather and everyday temperature reporting in the United States.

Kelvin (K)

The SI base unit for temperature, starting at absolute zero (0 K), the point where no lower temperature is possible. Used throughout science.
